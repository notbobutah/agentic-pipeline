# Travel Policy v4.1

Status: superseded by v4.2 on 2026-07-01. Retained for audit only.

## §3 Airfare

Airfare must be economy class for flights under 4 hours.

## §5 Lodging and meals

Meal per diem is $60/day for domestic travel.
Domestic hotel nightly cap is $180.

## §6 Ground transportation

Rental cars do not require prior approval.
