# Preferred Vendors Guide

Status: in force. Edition 2026-H2. Owner: Travel Desk.

## Page 1: Using this guide

Preferred vendors are recommended, not mandatory.
A negotiated rate is the price offered to the company. It is not a policy cap.
Travel Policy limits still apply to preferred vendors.

## Page 2: Chicago

Preferred hotel in Chicago is the Loop Grand, negotiated rate $210/night.

## Page 3: Other cities

There is no preferred hotel in Denver, Austin, or Toronto.

## Page 4: Booking problems

Travelers who cannot obtain a negotiated rate should contact the Travel Desk.
