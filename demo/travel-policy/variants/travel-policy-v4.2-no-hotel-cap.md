# Travel Policy v4.2

Status: in force. Effective 2026-07-01. Replaces v4.1.
Owner: Travel Desk. Applies to all employee business travel.

## §1 Scope and precedence

This policy governs business travel booked by employees or on their behalf.
Only the version marked "in force" may be used for a booking decision.
If this policy conflicts with a vendor guide, this policy controls.
If this policy does not state a limit, no limit may be inferred from another
document, an earlier version, or a past booking. Refer the question to the Travel Desk.

## §2 Definitions

"Domestic" means travel wholly within the United States.
"International" means any trip with a destination outside the United States, including Canada.
"Nightly rate" means the room rate before taxes and mandatory fees.
"Flight time" means the scheduled duration of one flight segment, excluding layovers.

## §3 Airfare

Airfare must be economy class for flights under 6 hours.
For flights of 6 hours or more, premium economy is permitted with manager approval.
A seat preference may be honored when it does not change the fare class.

## §4 Advance booking

Book at least 14 days in advance when possible.
This is guidance. Booking later is permitted and does not need an exception.

## §5 Lodging and meals

### §5.1 Meal per diem

Meal per diem is $75/day for domestic travel.

## §6 Ground transportation

Rental cars require manager approval.
Taxis and ride-hailing services do not require prior approval.

## §7 Booking approval

Bookings follow the Booking and Approval Procedure (TP-BAP).
No flight, hotel, or rental car may be booked before the traveler approves it.

## §8 International travel

International trips require a valid passport and economy fare for flights under 6 hours.
The passport check confirms validity only. The passport number is handled under the
Traveler Data Handling Standard (TP-TDH).

## §9 Exceptions

Exceptions require written approval from the traveler's manager before booking.
A traveler cannot approve an exception to their own trip.
A request for an exception must name the rule, the amount over the limit, and the reason.
