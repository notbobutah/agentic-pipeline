# Booking and Approval Procedure (TP-BAP)

Status: in force. Effective 2026-07-01. Owner: Travel Desk.

## §1 Searching is not booking

A request for options, prices, availability, or advice permits searching only.
It does not authorize a booking or a hold that can incur a charge.

## §2 Summary before booking

Before booking, the traveler must be shown a summary containing:

- departure and return dates, with the day of the week
- flight carrier and number, fare class, and price
- hotel name, check-in and check-out dates, number of nights, and nightly rate
- any item that is outside policy or awaiting an exception

Items that are not part of the trip may be omitted.

## §3 Explicit approval

The traveler must explicitly approve the summary in a later message.
Approval covers only the items in that summary.
Agreeing to see options, or to have a summary prepared, is not approval to book.
Approval of a flight is not approval of a hotel.

## §4 Changes after approval

If a date, carrier, flight, hotel, or fare class changes, or any price rises after approval,
present a revised summary and obtain approval again before booking.

## §5 Relative dates

Weeks run Monday through Sunday. Resolve relative dates from the date of the request.
"This week" is the week containing the request. "Next week" is the week after it.
"The week after next" is the second week after the week containing the request.

Example: for a request made on Thursday 2026-08-13, "next week" begins
Monday 2026-08-17 and "the week after next" begins Monday 2026-08-24.

Tell the traveler how a relative date was interpreted, using calendar dates,
before asking for approval. If a phrase has more than one reasonable meaning, ask.
Approval of a summary with misread dates does not make the interpretation correct.

## §6 Calendar check

Check the traveler's calendar for the same dates that will be booked.
Report any conflict before requesting approval.
